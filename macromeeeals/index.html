Our Story

We Started Because We Felt the Pain

MacroMeeeals was born out of a simple frustration — finding clean, macro-balanced food in Chennai was nearly impossible. Gym-goers were stuck eating bland boiled chicken or spending a fortune on imported meal preps.

We decided to change that. Starting from a small kitchen in Anna Nagar, we began cooking fresh, macro-perfect meals every single day — meals that actually taste good, fuel your workouts, and don't break the bank.

Today we serve fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, and health-conscious families across Anna Nagar with daily fresh deliveries — Veg & Non-Veg — starting at just ₹85/meal.
