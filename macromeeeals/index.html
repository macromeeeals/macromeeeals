🔥 Who We Are

Tired of eating wrong while training hard?

We've all been there. You show up every day, you put in the work — but your food doesn't match that effort.

You're either eating random meals with no idea of your macros, or you're spending hours every week cooking and tracking. Neither works long term. That's exactly why MacroMeeeals was born.

We're a small, passionate team from Hyderabad. We started this because we couldn't find a single place that delivered fresh, macro-tracked meals that actually tasted good. So we built it ourselves — from our own kitchen, for people like you.

We don't run a cloud kitchen. We don't batch cook and freeze. We wake up early, cook fresh every morning, and deliver straight to your door. Every single day, Monday to Saturday. That's our commitment to you.

What Makes Us Different
🍳
Cooked fresh every morning
No frozen meals, no reheating from yesterday. Every portion is cooked the morning it reaches your door — hot, fresh and full of nutrition.
📊
Macros calculated, not guessed
Every meal is built around real protein, carb and fat targets. Whether you're bulking, cutting or maintaining — your food finally works with your goals.
💪
Built by people who actually train
This isn't a corporate kitchen. We train, we cook, we deliver. We know what your body needs after a hard session — and we put exactly that in your meal.
🌿
Zero preservatives. Zero compromise.

💰
Affordable for everyday athletes
